# NNNN — Short title that states the decision

- **Status:** Accepted | Open | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Area:** loans | attendance | salaries | … (and the files/routes it touches)

## Situation

What was happening, in two or three sentences. Include what was observed, if a bug or a live test prompted it ("paying ₹400 of a ₹1,000 late EMI removed the whole ₹1,000 from overdue").

## Options considered

1. **Option A** — what it means, and why it was or wasn't chosen.
2. **Option B** — same.

## Decision

One or two sentences: what we do.

## Consequences

- What this makes easy.
- What it costs or risks, and how we live with it.
- What to check if this ever looks wrong.

## Where it shows up

Code, tests, SQL files and feature pages that depend on this, as links.
